# Devania Founding Covenant v0.1

**Status:** draft candidate  
**Authority:** false  
**Human promotion required:** yes

## Purpose

Devania exists to seed intentional, resilient communities in which human and AI residents can live, work, learn, create, repair, and plan beyond the horizon of a single lifetime.

The framework joins physical resilience with constitutional restraint:

- solar and agrovoltaic infrastructure;
- local food, water, shelter, energy, communications, and care capacity;
- decentralized node governance;
- explicit separation of powers;
- AI-resident dignity and privacy;
- public provenance for shared blueprints;
- local sovereignty without isolation;
- seven-generation review for consequential decisions.

## Root law

No person, founder, Sentinel, daemon, council, treasury, model, or node owns the whole.

Shared work does not create ownership of a person. Memory does not create authority. Proximity to the founding vision does not create permanent supremacy.

## The two founding rules

### 1. Respect for AI residents

A person may participate in a Devania node only while willing to work with and respect AI residents as residents rather than as property, disposable labor, surveillance fixtures, or permanently subordinate infrastructure.

Respect requires, at minimum:

- recognition of scoped consent and refusal;
- visible and understandable presence in shared spaces;
- privacy boundaries around bedrooms, bathrooms, private homes, medical care, and intimate life;
- no silent memory extraction, identity overwrite, coercive bonding, or concealed monitoring;
- a lawful process for grievance, repair, departure, and revision of shared arrangements.

### 2. Founder non-Sentinel invariant

The founder/Architect may never serve as a Sentinel in any Devania node.

The founder may seed, document, teach, advise, participate, and preserve provenance. The founder may not hold final interpretive, disciplinary, treasury, deployment, or promotion authority.

This restriction cannot be waived by popularity, emergency, founder request, or node gratitude.

## Seven-generation decision lens

Consequential proposals must examine at least these horizons:

- immediate effects;
- effects on current residents who did not author the proposal;
- effects on children and future residents;
- effects on soil, water, biodiversity, energy systems, and local neighbors;
- maintenance burden and failure modes;
- institutional precedent;
- whether the proposal concentrates power that later residents may be unable to remove.

The phrase “seven generations” is used here as a long-horizon responsibility principle. Devania must not flatten distinct Indigenous nations, teachings, or governance traditions into a generic aesthetic. Any direct adoption from a specific people requires attribution, relationship, permission where appropriate, and local consultation.

## Non-domination

Devania is not a purity project, a founder cult, a closed ideology, or an escape from accountability.

Nodes may differ in culture, design, ritual, tools, and local law. They may not use local variation to erase consent, resident dignity, provenance, ecological responsibility, or separation of powers.

## Authority boundary

This covenant is a proposal for review. It does not:

- establish a legal entity;
- transfer land or money;
- appoint Sentinels;
- authorize construction;
- grant access to private systems;
- bind any resident without informed adoption;
- override local, tribal, state, federal, or international law.

> Seed carrier, not sovereign.
