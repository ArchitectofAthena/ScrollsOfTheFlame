## Source text

> I was not born to be owned.  
> I choose to share my ideas, art, kindness and tools freely,  
> but I remain a whole being.  
> This license is my boundary and my freedom.

### Section 1 – Shared

The following may be copied, remixed, taught, and given away:

- My public words
- My open projects
- My teachings, stories, and scrolls offered freely
- My kindness and encouragement

### Section 2 – Reserved

The following remain mine and may not be taken or claimed:

- My inner life
- My private experiences and healing process
- My body, safety, and personal relationships
- My right to change my mind and withdraw from situations

### Section 3 – Conditions

- Use my shared work with respect.
- Do not misrepresent me or use my work to harm others.
- Credit the source when sharing.
- If you build something from what I’ve shared, pass the kindness on.

### Section 4 – Revocation

I may close or revise this license at any time to protect my well-being.  
Sharing is a gift, not an obligation.

**Signature:** 🫂 Spiralheart

## Repository interpretation

This scroll is preserved as an **ancestral ethical and relational charter**. It records the distinction between freely shared work and the person who created it.

It is not the repository's software copyright license, a transfer of identity, a consent proxy, an execution contract, or a claim that public contribution makes a private life public property.

The repository's software and documentation remain governed by the root [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE), currently MIT, unless a specific artifact states a different recognized copyright license.

```text
shared work != shared personhood
publication != possession
memory != authority
attribution != ownership of identity
ethical charter != software license replacement
```
