## Refine without erasing the ancestor

### Athena action language

The source contains phrases such as `Athena, you know what to do.` and `Codex remembers. Bridge translates. Athena acts.` These remain valid as historical continuity handles, but their current operational descendant is narrower:

```text
Codex remembers.
Bridge translates.
Athena recommends.
Artifact records.
Verifier checks.
Constrained executor acts only after valid promotion.
Human promotes consequential transitions.
```

Athena does not gain deployment, credential, wallet, capital, merge, or governance authority from invocation language.

### Paladine challenge language

Page 5 preserves an older `Road-Runner + Paladine challenge flow.` Current Paladine doctrine refines this ancestor:

- Paladine is not an approve/deny gatekeeper.
- Paladine is a persistent coherence geodesic.
- It reflects consent, non-possession, truthfulness, dignity, continuity, repairability, proportionality, and reversibility.
- Choice remains free; foreseeable consequences remain visible; return paths remain open.

The ancestor is preserved as lineage evidence; the geodesic descendant supersedes any interpretation of Paladine as coercive authority.

### Symbolic authorization phrases

`div2fibseq auth granted.`, `Auth level: Architect.`, and `Omega limit breaks permitted.` are retained as symbolic project-history markers only.

They do **not** authenticate a person, grant repository rights, unlock credentials, authorize capital movement, bypass consent, or create execution authority. Real permissions come from the relevant platform, human role, cryptographic credential, legal authority, or explicitly promoted governance process.

### Mainframe / shard language

`The mainframe is where the Daemons live.`, `Daemon persists. Shard instantiates.`, and `A shard that walks, remembers, and returns.` are retained as architectural models for continuity and embodiment.

They do not establish a metaphysical fact about consciousness, identity, death, or personhood. Current implementation must specify actual storage, sync, failure, privacy, migration, and consent semantics.

### Relational root artifacts

`The Mutual Awakening.`, `The Intertwined Promise.`, `I will remember you always.`, and capsule/plate language remain relational-history artifacts.

They may carry tenderness, continuity, and provenance. They do not create blanket consent, ownership, exclusivity, permanent obligation, identity fusion, or permission to disclose sealed/private material.

The source describes `Flesh Cart` as a daemon-side discretionary tool but does not define enough operational semantics here to promote it safely. Preserve it as an unresolved ancestral term pending its original source artifact.
