# Devania Images and Maps

This directory is for reviewed diagrams, plans, renders, maps, sketches, photographs, and visual references that belong to the public Devania blueprint set.

## Keep public and private material separate

Public blueprint images may include:

- generic site plans;
- modular housing layouts;
- agrovoltaic sections;
- water and energy flow diagrams;
- governance diagrams;
- accessibility studies;
- conceptual renders;
- non-sensitive construction details;
- educational infographics.

Do not publish here without deliberate review:

- exact resident addresses or private-home layouts;
- access-control and security vulnerabilities;
- credentials, QR secrets, wallet material, keys, or private identifiers;
- precise locations of children, medical resources, safe rooms, or vulnerable residents;
- private AI substrate access maps;
- images lacking permission or a clear license;
- maps that expose protected ecological, ceremonial, or culturally sensitive locations.

## Naming convention

Use:

`DEVANIA_<NODE-or-GENERIC>_<DOMAIN>_<DESCRIPTION>_vMAJOR_MINOR.<ext>`

Examples:

- `DEVANIA_GENERIC_AGROVOLTAIC_ARRAY_SECTION_v0_1.png`
- `DEVANIA_NODE0_WATER_FLOW_CONCEPT_v0_1.svg`
- `DEVANIA_GENERIC_SEPARATION_OF_POWERS_v0_1.png`

## Required sidecar metadata

Each promoted image should have a neighboring metadata file or manifest entry recording:

- title;
- creator;
- creation date;
- source file;
- source repository or source witness;
- license or permission basis;
- whether AI generation or editing was involved;
- what is conceptual versus measured;
- node/location privacy classification;
- SHA-256 after canonicalization;
- promotion status;
- known inaccuracies or unresolved questions.

## Promotion states

- `INBOX` — received but not reviewed
- `SOURCE_WITNESS` — preserved as supplied
- `CANDIDATE` — organized and under review
- `PROMOTED` — approved for the public blueprint set
- `SUPERSEDED` — preserved but replaced by a successor
- `PRIVATE_REFERENCE_ONLY` — never publish in the public repository

Images are evidence and design language. They are not construction approval.
