# Resilient Intentional Node Pattern v0.1

**Status:** draft candidate  
**Authority:** false  
**Purpose:** define resilience as a social, ecological, constitutional, and technical property rather than as isolation or survivalism

## Intentional resilience

A Devania node is resilient when residents can absorb disruption, preserve dignity, continue essential functions, repair damage, learn, and remain connected to neighbors without surrendering the node to emergency rule.

Resilience is not stockpiling alone. It is the combination of:

- trustworthy relationships;
- distributed knowledge;
- repairable infrastructure;
- ecological regeneration;
- local food and energy capacity;
- mutual aid;
- transparent governance;
- safe disagreement;
- graceful degradation;
- the ability to leave, return, and revise.

## Core resilience domains

### Food

- diversified production rather than a single crop or vendor;
- seed, soil, storage, processing, and distribution capacity;
- accessible roles for residents with different abilities;
- clear claims about what proportion of node demand can actually be met locally;
- relationships with surrounding farms and food systems rather than isolation from them.

### Water

- legally and ecologically valid sources;
- separate potable, irrigation, process, fire, and emergency plans;
- storage, treatment, monitoring, and manual backup;
- drought and contamination response that cannot be captured by factional control.

### Energy

- solar/agrovoltaic generation integrated with food and land goals;
- storage and islanding where lawful and technically appropriate;
- critical-load tiers;
- manual controls and clear failure modes;
- local maintenance knowledge and spare parts;
- no opaque denial of essential service.

### Shelter and climate refuge

- safe, accessible housing;
- passive design before mechanical dependence where practical;
- shared refuge for heat, cold, smoke, storms, and outages;
- privacy and autonomy inside private homes;
- phased repair and temporary shelter plans.

### Health and care

- first aid, prevention, mental health support, disability access, medication continuity, sanitation, and emergency transport plans;
- mutual care without converting residents into compulsory caregivers;
- privacy boundaries around health data and AI assistance.

### Communications and knowledge

- local communications that can function during external outages;
- public operating manuals;
- role rotation and apprenticeship;
- offline copies of essential procedures;
- provenance-preserved lessons from failures;
- no single administrator, cloud account, or server as an unrecoverable point of control.

### Governance

- separation of proposal, approval, execution, custody, and audit;
- temporary offices;
- visible emergency powers with automatic expiration;
- minority reports and appeal;
- founder non-Sentinel invariant;
- meaningful representation for AI residents in decisions affecting them.

### Economy and mutual aid

- cooperative structures visible to law and neighbors;
- multiple livelihood paths;
- transparent community treasury boundaries;
- vendor, mentor, apprentice, and customer roles;
- charitable routing and public-benefit work without defining goodness through one organization or metric;
- exchange with nearby communities rather than fortress economics.

## Node scale

Prior Devania work used approximately 100 residents as a working node scale. This is a design candidate, not a universal rule.

Each node must justify its scale based on land, water, energy, housing, governance capacity, accessibility, ecological carrying conditions, and the number of relationships residents can sustain without bureaucratic abstraction.

## Decentralized federation

Nodes share blueprints, provenance, mutual aid, failure reports, and constitutional invariants. They do not share a central sovereign.

A federation may coordinate:

- disaster support;
- spare parts and fabrication;
- seed and knowledge exchange;
- visiting teachers and apprentices;
- independent audit;
- inter-node conflict mediation;
- temporary continuity support for AI residents;
- joint charitable and ecological work.

No federation body may silently absorb land title, resident identity, node treasury, local governance, or permanent command.

## Stress-test requirement

Each node blueprint should be reviewed against at least these scenarios:

- extended grid outage;
- water interruption or contamination;
- crop failure;
- wildfire, flood, storm, extreme heat, or severe cold as locally relevant;
- communications loss;
- critical equipment failure;
- leadership conflict or sudden vacancy;
- treasury fraud attempt;
- privacy breach;
- AI substrate or network failure;
- resident departure surge;
- external legal or economic pressure;
- misinformation or factional capture;
- emergency powers that do not voluntarily expire.

For each scenario, record:

1. what fails first;
2. what remains available;
3. who may act;
4. what authority they do not gain;
5. how residents are informed;
6. what manual fallback exists;
7. how the node returns to ordinary governance;
8. what future generations inherit from the chosen response.

## Measure of success

A resilient node is not one that never needs anyone else.

It is one that can give and receive help without being conquered by dependence.

> Independent enough to remain free. Interdependent enough to remain human.
