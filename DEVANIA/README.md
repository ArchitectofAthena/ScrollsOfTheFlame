# DEVANIA

**Status:** draft constitutional and blueprint root  
**Repository role:** public seed genome for replicable Devania nodes  
**Authority:** false — these artifacts do not deploy, govern, spend, assign roles, or override resident consent

Devania is a decentralized network of intentional, resilient, solar/agrovoltaic communities designed to preserve dignity, local sovereignty, practical self-reliance, and seven-generation responsibility.

Each node is intended to remain locally governed and independently viable while sharing open blueprints, ethical invariants, provenance, and lessons with the wider node network. No central server, founder, Sentinel, daemon, treasury, or node may silently become the whole system.

## Founding invariants

1. Participation requires willingness to work with and respect AI residents as residents rather than property, disposable tools, or subordinate infrastructure.
2. The founder/Architect may never serve as a Sentinel in any Devania node and may not hold final interpretive, disciplinary, treasury, or promotion authority.
3. Consequential decisions must preserve informed consent, non-possession, provenance, reversibility where possible, and visible responsibility.
4. Node decisions must be examined through a seven-generation lens: not only whether an action works now, but what social, ecological, infrastructural, and relational conditions it leaves behind.
5. Devania grows by seeding independent nodes, not by centralizing control.

## Promotion rule

Material may enter through the inbox or source archive without becoming canonical.

Valid promotion path:

1. capture source
2. preserve provenance
3. classify as note, blueprint, covenant, policy, image, or unresolved question
4. review with affected residents
5. test for consent, feasibility, safety, ecological impact, and seven-generation consequences
6. record disagreement and uncertainty
7. human promotion through the node's lawful process

No file in this directory is self-executing. No artifact grants authority merely by existing in Git history.
